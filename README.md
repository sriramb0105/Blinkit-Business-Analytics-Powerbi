## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end Business Analytics solution for Blinkit using **Python**, **Pandas**, **Feature Engineering**, and **Microsoft Power BI**.

The objective is to transform raw business data into meaningful insights that support strategic decision-making across:

- 📈 Sales
- 👥 Customer Analytics
- 📦 Product Analytics
- 📢 Marketing Analytics
- 💬 Customer Feedback

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall business performance.
- Monitor sales trends and revenue.
- Understand customer purchasing behaviour.
- Evaluate product performance.
- Measure marketing campaign effectiveness.
- Analyze customer satisfaction.
- Build executive dashboards for management.
